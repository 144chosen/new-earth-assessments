LOCATION LIMITS
Aramatena, Aveyon, Tara, density levels, councils, and other realm names are presented as locations within the teachings’ cosmology. Their inclusion is not a finding of inhabited planets by modern astronomy. Sirius B, Vega, Lyra, Alcyone, and other astronomical names can overlap with the spiritual map without validating all claims in that map.

Aethien: the founder passage associates it with Lyra-Vega; Angelic Realities printed p. xxix independently names the illustrated Guardian expression as Gold Order, Mantis, Pegasus System. Both contexts are distinguished, and a separate exact planet is not fabricated.
Pegasai: a Pegasus-like bodily form does not itself establish the Pegasus star system as its origin. The checked founder passage says Lyra-Aveyon.
Specialized feline/cetacean Oraphim: no distinct exact planet was established in the checked passages, so their location notes retain that uncertainty.
Yanas: descriptions beyond the Time Matrix do not support a physical planetary birthplace.

SCORING
Heritage: 30 five-point self-report questions measure ten NEU reflection themes. Theme means are scaled to 0-100. Each illustrated profile receives a weighted mean using its declared theme weights. If a single Sun element can be established, a small NEU interpretation contributes 2%; otherwise no astrological weighting is applied. A profile can change by at most two points due to this layer. Unknown birth times that cross a Sun-sign boundary are not assigned one Sun element.

The top three independent scores are displayed. They do not sum to 100 and are not ancestry probabilities. Ties have deterministic ordering and are described as overlapping / broad results. Related expressions can naturally score closely. The content does not promise psychometric validation, a fixed soul destiny, or a proven star-system ancestry.

Purpose: 20 current questions rank ten service domains; domain scores combine 97% current answers and 3% earlier heritage answer themes. The strongest three domains expose six specific role questions each, for 18 further questions. Role scores combine 60% domain score and 40% current role preference. All 60 roles can be the primary result. Career suggestions and plans reflect the self-reported role pattern and practical context; they do not establish that a person is already qualified.

Career directions cover all supplied roles, with three options per role (180 entries before deduplication). Top-three results receive relevant options; the paid results screen also provides the complete role/career catalogue. Spiritual examples include ceremony and celebrancy, meditation facilitation, coaching and purpose reflection, sound/music practice, contemplative teaching, movement, retreat work, spiritual writing and art, grief companionship, and nature-based facilitation. Conventional and adjacent roles include events, training, design, administration, community services, environmental work, skilled building, care coordination, communications, and research.
